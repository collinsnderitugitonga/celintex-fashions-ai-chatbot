================================================== 10. MEASUREMENTS
==================================================

Accurate measurements are important for customized clothing and wedding
gown hire.

Measurement Location: Accra Road, Scorpio Building, Nairobi CBD.

Measurement Procedure:

1.  Book a Measurement Appointment

-   Customers should schedule a measurement appointment with Celintex
    where required.
-   Customers should arrive on time.

2.  Prepare for Measurement

-   Wear comfortable, fitted clothing where possible.
-   For wedding gowns or formal wear, customers should bring or wear the
    undergarments and shoes intended for the outfit where practical.

3.  Professional Measurement

-   Measurements should be taken by a Celintex tailor or authorized
    staff member.
-   A proper measuring tape should be used.
-   The tape should be placed comfortably, not excessively tight or
    loose.

4.  Possible Measurements Depending on the garment, measurements may

-   Bust/chest
-   Underbust
-   Waist
-   Hips
-   Shoulder width
-   Neck
-   Arm/bicep
-   Sleeve length
-   Dress/garment length
-   Back length
-   Trouser waist
-   Thigh
-   Knee
-   Inseam
-   Height

5.  Measurement Verification

-   Important measurements should be checked before recording.
-   The customer should confirm that the recorded measurements are
    theirs before production or alteration begins.

6.  Special Garments Wedding gowns, men’s suits, trousers, bridesmaid
    dresses, and other customized garments may require additional
    measurements depending on the design.

The tailor determines which measurements are necessary.

7.  Measurement Changes Customers should inform Celintex if their body
    measurements change significantly before final fitting. Additional
    adjustments may be required.

The AI must not invent garment-specific measurement requirements.

If a customer asks: “Can I send my measurements?”

================================================== 11. FITTING
APPOINTMENT PROCEDURE ==================================================

1.  Schedule the fitting.
2.  Arrive on time.
3.  Bring relevant shoes, undergarments, shirts, or accessories where
    they may affect the fit.
4.  Try on the garment under tailor supervision.
5.  Assess fit, length, comfort, and overall appearance.
6.  Identify required adjustments.
7.  Approved alterations are carried out by Celintex staff or authorized
    personnel.
8.  A second or additional fitting may be scheduled if required.
9.  Attend the final fitting.
10. Inspect the garment before collection and report concerns
    immediately.

Customers must not make alterations themselves.

For wedding outfits, the final fitting should ideally occur sufficiently
before the wedding date to allow necessary adjustments.

================================================== 12. CUSTOMER-SUPPLIED
FABRIC POLICY ==================================================

Celintex may accept customer-supplied fabric subject to inspection and
suitability.

1.  Fabric Inspection

-   Celintex will inspect the fabric before accepting it for production.
-   The tailor may assess condition, quality, size, colour, stretch, and
    suitability for the requested design.

2.  Customer Responsibility

-   The customer is responsible for providing sufficient fabric.
-   Celintex may advise on the approximate amount required.
-   The customer should provide the agreed fabric.

3.  Fabric Suitability

-   Celintex may advise that supplied fabric is unsuitable.
-   Some designs require particular fabric characteristics such as
    structure, weight, or stretch.

4.  Fabric Condition Celintex is not responsible for pre-existing
    defects including:

-   Stains
-   Fading
-   Weak areas
-   Holes
-   Discolouration
-   Manufacturing defects

Noticeable defects should be identified and recorded before cutting.

5.  Cutting and Wastage

-   Once fabric is cut, it may not be possible to return it to its
    original form.
-   Some fabric may remain unused.
-   Cutting should begin only after design and measurements are
    approved.

6.  Insufficient Fabric If fabric is insufficient, the customer may need
    to:

-   Provide additional fabric, or
-   Agree to a design modification.

7.  Colour and Material Variations Celintex is not responsible for
    variations already present in customer-supplied fabric. Additional
    fabric may have differences in colour, texture, or dye lot.

8.  Fabric Handling Celintex will take reasonable care when handling
    customer-supplied fabric.

9.  Leftover Fabric Significant unused fabric should be returned upon
    collection unless otherwise agreed. Small scraps or cutting waste
    may not be suitable for return.

10. Customer Approval The customer should confirm design, measurements,
    and fabric suitability before cutting begins.

Changes after cutting may attract additional charges or may not be
possible.

AI RULE: The AI must not assume how much fabric is required, whether
damaged fabric can be used, or whether a specific fabric is suitable
without assessment by Celintex.

================================================== 17. RETURNS AND
EXCHANGES ==================================================

GENERAL RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY

Eligible ready-made products can generally be returned or exchanged
within 7 days of purchase.

Returned products should be: - Unworn - Unused - In original condition -
Free from stains - Free from damage - Free from alterations - Unwashed -
Returned with original packaging and tags - Accompanied by receipt or
other proof of purchase

Customers should contact Celintex before returning an item.

EXCHANGES: Eligible products may be exchanged for: - Different size -
Different available colour - Another product of the same value, subject
to availability

If the replacement costs more, the customer pays the difference.

If the replacement costs less, the applicable balance is handled
according to Celintex refund procedures.

NON-RETURNABLE PRODUCTS: Generally not eligible for return or
exchange: - Customized clothing - Made-to-measure clothing -
Personalized products - Altered garments - Used or washed clothing -
Products damaged by the customer - Opened or used grooming/personal-care
products - Opened or used fragrances

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS: If a customer receives a damaged,
defective, or incorrect product, they should contact Celintex as soon as
possible.

Celintex may request photographs or other information.

If the issue is confirmed to be a Celintex error or product defect,
Celintex may offer: - Replacement - Exchange - Another appropriate
solution

RETURN COSTS: For customer-initiated returns such as change of mind or
incorrect size selection, the customer may be responsible for applicable
return or delivery costs.

If the product is defective, damaged, or incorrect due to Celintex
error, Celintex may cover applicable return or replacement costs.

REFUNDS: Approved refunds are processed according to Celintex’s payment
and refund procedures. Where possible, refunds may be made through the
original payment method.
